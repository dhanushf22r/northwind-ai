# Week 1 Boss Fight: Daniel Osei, Intro Call

**Date:** Sun 25 Oct 2026 · **Jira:** KAN-14 · **Character:** Daniel Osei, CEO
**Result:** C+ (pass) · **XP:** +6 (10 for the win, −4 for heavy hints) · **Total XP after:** 29

## Setup

- **Scene:** Monday, 9:02 a.m., the glass meeting room. "NEVER AGAIN" on the whiteboard. Daniel has 10 minutes before the board pre-read. Helen Brandt (CFO) sits in the corner taking notes.
- **Win condition:** get Daniel to name **one number** he wants to change.
- **Hint card:** ask about outcomes, not technology. One question at a time.

## Transcript

My lines are verbatim. Daniel's lines are summarised.

**Daniel:** Three consultancies in 18 months, all shiny, none changed anything. Before you show me anything: what do you actually need from me?

**Me:** "Need all the data and I want to know how all teams function. In that way I can try connecting the dots. For example - the last chatbot said the pallet had left the city when it was somewhere else - which means it hallucinated, so the main thing in our project is no halluciantion, we're gonna build somehting that really works - for that i need the time and data tounderstand how proccesses work here"

**Daniel:** Likes "something that really works". Pushes back: all the data, all the teams and time is what the last three asked for. Tom won't hand over "all the data". Confirms the Harbour Foods / Rotterdam story. "You haven't asked me a single thing about what *I* need for the board."

**Me:** "You have a board meeting? what would you need?"

**Daniel:** Told the board in March that Northwind goes AI-first. Needs a story: lost Harbour Foods over slow support, fixed it with AI. "They'll want to know it was worth the money." *(Helen's pen stops; she looks at Daniel.)* Quickly: "Support first."

**Me:** "Can you let me know how many customer support agents were there who were let go because of AI?"

**Daniel:** None; there is no AI yet. Warns that layoff talk would spook Sofia's 40 drowning agents. "Not *this* part of it, anyway." *(Helen closes her notebook halfway and watches me.)*

*(Help call 1: told to quantify one of Daniel's own phrases; watch Helen.)*

**Me:** "I'll build somehting which eases customer support- an app which actually does work and makes the customer happy. once I understand all the processes I am going to make sure I will budget it, cost optimise a solution and give you a good product and will keep you updated on the progress every week"

**Daniel:** Updates on *what*? "The board doesn't read adjectives. They read numbers." Stands to leave: "Ask me the question."

*(Help call 2: question frame given. Help call 3: told the number is in the prologue: 9 days.)*

**Me:** "When you say tickets need to be replied quick and correctly. -where it took 9 days for the bot to tell an icorrect answer, what number would show the board it worked, and what's that number today?"

**Daniel:** Corrects me: the bot answered in seconds and was wrong; the *people* took 9 days to fix it. Today's average first response is **4 days**, worst case 9. Target: **under 24 hours**, and right answers. Writes **"4 days → < 24 hrs"** under NEVER AGAIN. On the way out: "There's another number the board will ask about. Probably with Helen."

Needed 3 help calls; the last one gave away the number.

## What I missed

1. **Got the story wrong.** The bot answered instantly and wrongly; humans took 9 days to fix it. Daniel corrected me in front of the CFO.
2. **No deadline.** "Under 24 hours" by when? Should have asked "By when?"
3. **"Right answers" not pinned down.** A second metric, accuracy, left unmeasured.
4. **The other number.** Daniel dangled it on his way out ("probably with Helen"). Should have asked "What's the other number?"
5. **Helen.** Reacted to money three times and stayed behind. An open door I didn't use.

## One thing to practise

**Ask, then stop talking.** Two good questions got buried under pitches. End the turn on the question.

## Unlocked

- **North Star metric:** average first response time, **4 days → under 24 hours**.
- **Open thread:** Helen and "the other number".
